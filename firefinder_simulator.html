How to Use the Simulator

Go Fullscreen by clicking the expand icon. ()
Select a Lookout station and then choose a Scenario to begin training.
If you see smoke, use the Binoculars to get a close up view.
Use the Firefinder to determine the azimuth.
The Map will help you determine the distance and legal description.
Complete the Report form. Submit it to receive an evaluation.
