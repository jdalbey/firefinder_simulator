## Firefinder Simulator Screens

![screen 1](Screenshot%201.png)

The opening page of the Firefinder Simulator. A scenario is chosen using the dropdown box at the top left.

![screen 1](Screenshot%202.png)

When a scenario is chosen, a landscape photo appears with the view from the lookout. The objective is to identify a smoke.

![screen 3](Screenshot%203.png)

The Binoculars function shows a closeup photo of the smoke.

![screen 4](Screenshot%204.png)

The Firefinder function displays a replica of the sight on the firefinder.

![screen 5](Screenshot%205.png)

The firefinder sight can be moved to align with the smoke, and the azimuth read from the scale at the bottom.

![screen 6](Screenshot%206.png)

The map function displays a map with the red line matching the firefinder sightline.

![screen 7](Screenshot%207.png)

The map can be zoomed, and a PLSS layer added to determine the legal description.

![screen 8](Screenshot%208.png)

The user completes a smoke report form with the information they have gathered. When the form is submitted, their responses receive an evaluation (correct, incorrect).

The simulator has multiple scenarios available of varying difficulties.
